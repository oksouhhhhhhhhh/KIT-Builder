Size Guide

  • Items with 3 characters use 65px.
  • Items with 4 characters use 63px.
  • Items with 5 characters use 62px.
  • Items with 6 characters use 60px.
  • Items with 7 characters use 61px.
  • Items with 8 characters use 62px. (Approximately)
  • Items with 9 characters use 58px. (Approximately)
  • Items with 10 characters use 52px. (Approximately)
  • Items with 11 characters use 47px. (Approximately)
  • Items with 12 characters use 43px. (Approximately)
  • Items with 13 characters use 40px. (Approximately)
  • Items with 14 characters use 37px.
  • Items with 15 characters use 34px.
  • Items with 16 characters use 31.5px.
  • Items with 17 characters use 30.75px.
  • Items with 18 characters use 32px.
